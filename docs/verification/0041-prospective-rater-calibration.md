## Prospective rater-persona clarification

Apply the following only when preparing **new, independently approved labels before a future evaluation**. It clarifies the [0036 rater persona](0036-rater-persona.md) and the existing [contract 3.2 anchors](../../src/model-contracts.js); it does not amend either historical artifact or define replacement ranges for the 20 reviewed cases.

1. **Relevance:** Score the requested action and audience, not topic words alone. Level 3 can cover one central part of a compound question concretely while another part is absent. Level 2 covers a related subtopic, safeguard or preparatory step that does not perform the named task. Level 1 has no meaningful answer to it. Do not require incidental context such as a city name unless the question actually asks about it.
2. **Support:** Credit question-related reasoning or concrete actions independently of how many requested parts were covered. A specific diagnostic step or example can justify level 3 without a complete result; level 4 needs a defensible reasoned example or trade-off, not an entire answer to every subquestion. A bare relevant assertion is level 2. Concrete detail aimed at a different task is at most level 2, and level 1 when it supports no question-related point. State which point the evidence supports.
3. **Structure:** Score the transcript's own organization, including off-topic or incomplete answers. Level 4 shows a clear causal, contrastive or stepwise progression that develops an idea, even briefly. Level 3 is understandable but thin, such as one claim with a simple reason or two merely related statements. Level 2 has weak links, fragmentation or repetition that impedes the line of thought; level 1 has no discernible relation or progression. Do not demand a task-specific plan, conclusion, minimum length or factual correctness to award structure.
4. **English expression:** Level 4 allows an isolated stylistic preference or mildly unusual phrase when the English remains natural overall and meaning is clear. Level 3 fits a noticeable grammar/word-choice problem, a hesitation that makes the wording noticeably awkward, or a brief untranslated phrase that does not obscure the main meaning; levels 2 and 1 retain the contract's meaning-barrier anchors. Quote the exact wording that makes a lower expression score warranted. Irrelevance, unsupported facts, implausibility and answer organization are scored elsewhere.
5. **Range discipline:** Prefer one level. Use an adjacent two-level range only when two readings both satisfy the written anchor on the **unseen** transcript; record the reason for each endpoint and obtain independent approval before inspecting model feedback. Never widen a historical range after seeing the result. If the same reviewed transcripts are reused, this post-run guide cannot make their new labels blind; use fresh held-out examples for calibration and preserve the contract-3.2 comparison as failed.

The existing 3.2 model outputs, AI-approved labels, validators and release thresholds remain unchanged. The formal label gate still fails 35/60 outputs, and the creator/persona gate is pending, so release remains **BLOCKED**. No new subscription evaluation is authorized by this document.
