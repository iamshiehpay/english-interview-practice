# Issue 0041: path to a trustworthy scoring release

## What the saved evidence rules out

The [current mismatch map](0041-rating-mismatch-map.md) shows **40/60 official contract-3.3 outputs** failing at least one frozen pre-feedback rating range. These are 40 outputs, not 40 structure decisions or 40 adjudication handoffs. Across those outputs there are 59 dimension misses: relevance 11, support 17, structure 27 (26 below the approved range), and English expression 4. Dimensions overlap within an output. Ten of twenty cases fail on all three repeats. Recomputing a hypothetical median score from the three saved repeats (without changing any output or release result) yields only **9/20** cases whose four median levels all match their approved ranges. Repetition or majority voting alone cannot resolve the systematic boundary differences. Fixed-input 3.4/3.5 diagnostics also show that switching models or appending prompt clarifications moves misses among dimensions rather than reliably clearing the gate. The 3.5 cases were reused during prompt drafting, so further tuning on them is not independent validation.

The product's [domain definition](../../CONTEXT.md) and [ADR 0006](../adr/0006-use-a-fixed-evidence-based-feedback-contract.md) require four separate, transcript-grounded ratings. [ADR 0016](../adr/0016-use-bilingual-feedback-with-shared-evidence.md) requires paired explanations with one shared set of levels and original quotes. [ADR 0020](../adr/0020-validate-v1-with-ai-persona-loops.md) permits independently reviewed AI labels for v1.0.0 while keeping deterministic constraints. Removing a rating, changing saved outputs, retrospectively changing labels, or widening the existing gate would not be a valid fix.

## One integrated scoring change

The structure 3/4 boundary is the largest cluster, but changing it alone cannot clear the 40/60 output-level failure. The next versioned scoring design must handle all four dimensions together while keeping their evidence separate:

| Dimension | Historical misses | Prospective decision evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Relevance | 11 | Identify each requested part and the transcript passage that substantively answers it; topic words or promises do not count as an answer. |
| Support | 17 | Identify the quoted action or reason and whether a concrete consequence or example actually supports it; do not infer a result absent from the transcript. |
| Structure | 27 | Apply a written, reproducible 3/4 organization boundary to connections *within* the transcript; do not deduct for missing requested content or missing outcomes that belong to another dimension. |
| English expression | 4 | Identify the exact wording and the language issue, independent of task fit, evidence quality, or answer organization. |

The separate bilingual gate also has **1/60** historical semantic failure. English and Traditional-Chinese explanations must describe the same action and timing from one shared assessment, with independent semantic review. A new run succeeds only if its own frozen-label comparison and every other applicable release gate pass under the unchanged acceptance rules; it does not retroactively turn the old 40/60 or 1/60 failures into passes.

## Prospective structure decision rule

This is a candidate rule derived from the saved evidence and the separately frozen [blind adjudication](0041-structure-boundary-review-handoff.md), not a relabeling of the old packet. Apply it to the transcript's own organization before reading the answer's task coverage, factual support or English quality:

1. Mark the clauses or short spans that carry distinct ideas, then name the relationship between them. Ignore whether the ideas answer the question, are true, have a completed result, or use polished English.
2. Award **4** when at least two moves form a developed causal, contrastive or stepwise line: a later move tests, narrows, qualifies or acts on an earlier one. A symptom → check → further inspection, or measurement → change → constraint, qualifies even if the result is absent. A single sentence may contain this progression; length alone is not a criterion.
3. Award **3** when a line is understandable but thin: one claim and a simple reason, two related points, or an action with a local explanation that does not develop the relationship across the answer. A causal word such as “because” alone does not force 4. If one of two safeguards has a reason but the safeguards are merely adjacent, the whole line remains 3.
4. Award **2** when shifts, weak links, fragmentation or repetition impede the line of thought, even if individual clauses are clear. Award **1** when no relationship or organizing line between the ideas is discernible. Neither level is decided by topic fit or grammar.

Before finalizing the level, record the exact transcript spans that establish the claimed relationship and one sentence explaining how the later span advances the earlier one. If that advancement cannot be stated from the transcript, do not assign 4. For example, the blind panel called the test → duplicate request → idempotency check → limitation account a 4; it called two related payment safeguards, only one with its own reason, a 3. These old examples illustrate the rule only; **new** blind inputs must test whether independent raters can apply it consistently.

## Evidence-first candidate shape

The next contract should keep the existing four user-facing ratings, quotes, bilingual reasons, strength and priority improvement. It should add a machine-checkable decision record for each rating before choosing the level: `relevance` names requested parts and exact answering spans; `support` names the question-related point, its exact supporting span and the claimed mechanism or consequence; `structure` records its exact linked spans, link type and advancement; `englishExpression` records the exact problematic wording and its effect on meaning, or explicitly records no material language issue. Categorical decisions must map to the four existing anchors. Targeted validation must reject empty or non-source quotes and impossible category/level combinations; independent review must catch semantically unrelated or misleading evidence. Automatic checks can establish traceability and internal consistency, **not** the semantic truth of a model judgment; fresh blind ratings and independent bilingual review remain necessary.

The production contract, provider JSON schema, domain validator and evaluation checker all currently enforce the old exact shape. Keep this design in a new versioned candidate and evaluation namespace until it passes prospective blind diagnosis; promotion must update those four boundaries together and preserve compatibility with old saved Feedback Reports. The shared English/Traditional-Chinese explanation must preserve actor, action, certainty, timing and advice. The historical 3.3 evaluator and artifacts stay untouched.

## Prospective repair, with stop points

1. **Adjudicate the rubric before new feedback.** The approved three-reviewer blind structure diagnosis is complete. Use its frozen decisions to write a prospective, operational structure 3/4 rule, including examples for recurring under-scoring and genuinely disputed boundaries. Check the other three dimensions against the saved mismatch map so the rule set covers all 40 failing outputs. This is diagnosis; historical labels and release results remain immutable. If a boundary cannot be applied consistently, resolve the rubric as a separate product decision before spending on another app evaluation.
2. **Make a versioned scoring design that exposes its evidence.** For each dimension, require observable, concise decision fields before selecting a level: which requested parts were substantively answered; which quoted action/reason has a defensible consequence; which *transcript-internal* statements are connected causally, contrastively or stepwise; and which exact wording causes an English-expression issue. A missing result or another unanswered requested part must not silently lower structure. Use a fixed mapping from these fields to levels where the rubric actually permits one. Keep the original transcript quotes and one shared set of ratings; produce English and Traditional-Chinese explanations from that same assessment. This is a prospective new contract, not post-processing of saved outputs to force a pass.
3. **Verify the design on new blind cases.** Freeze fresh questions/transcripts and independent AI Role-persona approvals before generating any new feedback. Use targeted tests for field/schema/quote integrity and dimensional separation, then a separately capped small diagnostic with independent bilingual review. Do not reuse the eight 3.4/3.5 development cases as a claimed held-out result. If the diagnostic fails, preserve the evidence and stop rather than relabel or rerun the failed cases.
4. **Run the unchanged official release gate only after a promising blind diagnostic.** A separately approved app evaluation must produce its own frozen analysis/label packet, sixty feedback outputs, automatic and stability checks, independent bilingual semantic review, and required AI-persona validation for changed production behavior. The 3.3 `BLOCKED` report remains the release decision until a new versioned report passes every applicable gate.

The first structure stop point has now been completed under a separate three-reviewer approval. The [blind structure-boundary handoff](0041-structure-boundary-review-handoff.md) finds both recurring model under-scoring and reproducible disagreement with five historical single-level structure approvals. This supports a **prospective** structure decision rule, integrated with the other three dimensions, and a new blind packet; it does not justify changing old labels or starting a full release run now.

This design is a hypothesis, not a guarantee of 60/60 exact agreement. Semantic scoring retains judgment, and even a passing finite run would not prove zero future errors. Its value is that failures become attributable to a named evidence decision or a genuinely ambiguous rubric boundary rather than another unstructured prompt tweak. No model request or production behavior change is authorized by this document; any additional independent AI review or new subscription feedback needs a separate concrete cap and user approval.
