Automated evidence: `node --test test/recording-limits.test.js` — 3/3 pass.
`npm test` — 118/118 pass. `npm run test:browser` — PASS, including the new
recording walk with a synthetic microphone.

A real defect was found and fixed during browser verification: reopening a
Practice Record whose stored transcript draft was left over from an earlier
recording disabled the submit button until the learner answered a
replace-or-keep prompt. Submission is no longer gated on that choice, and a
draft that already contains the transcript is not offered it again.

Limits and what is **not** verified:

- The synthetic `MediaRecorder` and `getUserMedia` in `test/browser-smoke.js`
  produce a fixed 2 KB blob. This proves the recording workflow, never audio
  capture, encoding, or transcription accuracy.
- The demonstration speech provider returns one fixed English sentence
  regardless of the audio, so no browser check here is evidence about
  transcription.
- **Pending human verification with a real microphone and a configured
  provider:** technical vocabulary, mixed Chinese and English, a genuine
  three-minute answer, a declined microphone permission, and a real
  transcription failure. The near-limit warning and the self-stop at 180 s were
  exercised through the constants and the timer, not by speaking for three
  minutes.
- Phone-browser recording is out of scope this round (desktop first).
- Recordings are still deleted after transcription; local retention and playback
  arrive in issue 0016.
