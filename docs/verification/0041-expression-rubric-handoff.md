# 0041 — English expression rubric and evidence

The frozen Codex v3 run has eight English-expression ratings of 2 outside the independently AI-approved 3–4 ranges: `ai-experience-depth` repeats 1 and 3, `backend-behavioral` repeats 1–3, and `embedded-technical-communication` repeats 1–3. In every affected output, relevance, support and structure are already 1. The English-expression reasons then cite missing answers, unsupported claims, implausibility or lack of organization. The same content defect is counted again under expression. Repeat 2 of `ai-experience-depth` rates expression 3 for the same transcript, which further supports a rubric ambiguity rather than a schema failure. These are AI-reviewed labels, not human ground truth or proof that a changed prompt will score correctly.

The operational rubric is: relevance measures response to the question; support measures evidence or reasoning; structure measures progression; English expression measures intelligibility, grammar, word choice and natural spoken English. Context may affect expression only when the wording itself is unclear or awkward. An irrelevant or unsupported answer can therefore have low relevance/support and high expression. Substantial untranslated language or transcription noise can lower expression when it obscures meaning. The model must cite the specific language issue, without crediting unsupported claims or adding facts.

Contract 3.1.0 states that boundary in the feedback prompt. It invalidates old contract-bound inputs for new evaluations. The frozen 3.0.0 results, labels, reviews and failed-run artifacts remain untouched. The offline reviewer uses their recorded contract version and original checksums, reports the model-contract source change, and still blocks the historical release on the same eight mismatches. No new model request was made. A new live assessment, if approved later, needs fresh output and independent semantic/label review; the prompt change alone is implementation evidence, not a release pass.

Until 0040 gives the evaluator a distinct artifact path, `evaluation/run.js` fails before provider setup under contract 3.1.0. This prevents any accidental overwrite of the frozen v3 evidence.
