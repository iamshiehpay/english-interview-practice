Independent review findings and what was done:

1. **Confirmed — overlapping playback and an object-URL leak.** The replay control
   was never disabled, and the speed `<select>`'s change handler re-enabled play
   mid-fetch, so two readings could start at once; the loser's object URL was
   overwritten in the cache without being revoked. Fixed with a single per-mount
   `loading` guard consulted by play, replay and the speed control, a cache-race
   check that revokes the losing URL, and a browser-smoke assertion that a burst of
   eight rapid clicks starts at most one further reading.
2. **Confirmed — Key-Sentence Corrections inside the collapsed follow-up history had
   no read-aloud control**, because that markup is static and never mounted. Fixed by
   tagging the history area with its attempt id and mounting it in `showRecord`.
3. **Plausible — the concurrency-bound 429 fell through to the generic Chinese
   fallback.** Fixed with explicit `localizeError` rules for the read-aloud refusals.
4. **Plausible — changing the speed on an idle control stopped another control's
   playback.** Fixed: the speed handler now stops only its own playback.
5. **Plausible — the Latin-script check was weaker than its comment implied.**
   Strengthened to also refuse text that is mostly Han while still allowing an
   English question that quotes a short Chinese resume line; both cases are tested.

The reviewer also noted that the smoke test's `document.querySelector('audio')`
check is weak on its own, since `new Audio()` is never appended to the DOM. That is
correct; the real no-autoplay evidence is the `window.__played` tracking beside it.

Automated evidence: `node --test test/read-aloud.test.js` — 7/7 pass.
`npm test` — 118/118 pass (108 before this issue; the extra three come from issue 0015).
`npm run test:browser` — PASS, including the new read-aloud assertions.
`npm run evaluate` — `automatedPass: true`, stability 80/80. `releaseStatus`
remains `BLOCKED` for the four pre-existing reasons (pending independent
bilingual semantic review, pending human labels, pending creator five-loop
validation, no live-model quality evaluation); this issue neither adds nor
removes a blocker.

Limits and what is **not** verified:

- The demonstration provider returns a 440 Hz tone, not speech. Every browser
  check above proves the read-aloud plumbing, never speech quality, pacing or
  intelligibility.
- No live OpenAI `gpt-4o-mini-tts` call was made. Real-voice acceptance, the
  usefulness of the three speeds, and provider cost per reading are pending human
  verification with a configured key.
- `HTMLMediaElement.prototype.play` is stubbed in the smoke test, so actual audio
  output through the browser's audio stack is unverified.
- Read-aloud deliberately sits outside the long-running operations tracker, so a
  reading cannot be cancelled from the operations strip; stopping playback is the
  learner's control and an in-flight request is bounded only by the provider.
- Corrections rendered inside the collapsed follow-up history (`followUpHistoryHtml`)
  show no read-aloud control; only the live corrections panel mounts one.
