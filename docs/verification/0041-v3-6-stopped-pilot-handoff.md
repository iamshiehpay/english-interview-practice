Completeness already failed at request four, and one of the three valid outputs missed two approved dimensions. The pilot is therefore **NO_GO** without the remaining requests or semantic review. Bilingual fidelity and evidence faithfulness for this partial set are **NOT_REVIEWED**; no pass or failure is inferred for them.

Any prospective successor should resolve the distinction between a related team interest and an actual requested skill, and between a general reason and concrete support. The evidence shape could also allow a related quote alongside a partial core answer while explicitly prohibiting it from counting as coverage. These are design hypotheses from a small development-set diagnostic, not a retrospective fix: do not revalidate the fourth output, change the frozen labels or rubric, resume the 3.6 run, or use these eight cases as a new held-out set. A successor needs a reviewed implementation, fresh blind inputs and labels frozen before feedback, and a separate concrete model-usage approval.
