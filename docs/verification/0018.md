Automated evidence: `node --test test/mock-sessions.test.js` — 10/10 pass.
`npm test` — 144/144 pass. `npm run test:browser` — PASS, walking a full session:
answer, record and submit by voice, skip, reach the summary, open one question's
feedback, and confirm the session appears under its job.

Two defects were found and fixed during implementation:

- An idempotent resubmit was rejected by the "answer in order" guard, because the
  guard ran before the submission-identifier replay check. A learner retrying
  their own submission after a dropped response would have been told to answer in
  order. The replay check now runs first.
- The Records view renders from a cached workspace that a session can move on
  from, so a just-finished session did not appear under its job until a reload.
  `showMockSession` now refreshes the cache.

Limits and what is **not** verified:

- Whether three questions in a row actually feels like a screening call, and
  whether the Session Summary is useful coaching rather than a platitude, are
  human judgements. The demonstration provider returns a fixed, labelled summary,
  so no browser check here is evidence about coaching quality. **Pending learner
  acceptance.**
- Per-question feedback, corrections and the English rewrite reuse the existing
  validated contracts unchanged; their quality is equally unverified here.
- Sessions deliberately contribute no evidence to Recurring Weakness tracking.
  Whether they eventually should is left open in the PRD.
- No preparation countdown and no follow-ups inside a session, as specified.
