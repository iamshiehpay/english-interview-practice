# Use deterministic practice workflows and bounded agentic research

The MVP will use an explicit, human-controlled workflow for discovery, job selection, analysis, question generation, answering, feedback, and revision. A second-stage Bounded Research Run may autonomously plan read-only searches for a selected role and produce a cited Role Research Brief, but it must operate under tool, source, step, time, and cost limits; it cannot modify Job Snapshots, apply to jobs, or choose career goals for the learner.
