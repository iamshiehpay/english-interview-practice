# Separate question coverage from experience grounding

Job Capability Maps determine what the learner may practise regardless of prior experience; the Candidate Evidence Profile is optional and only personalizes answer framing or prevents the coach from inventing past achievements. When a capability is an Experience Gap, the coach still asks representative questions and supports conceptual answers, hypothetical approaches, adjacent transferable experience, and honest learning plans. Missing Approved Evidence must never suppress a question or make a learner ineligible for the product.
